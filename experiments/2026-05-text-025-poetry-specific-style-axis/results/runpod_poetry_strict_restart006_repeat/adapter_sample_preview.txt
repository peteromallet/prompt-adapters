All things are dear to me, and some more dear;
And I must lead my life, though every step is doubt.

One day one way that seemed the quickest may
Have led me through a field of blinding mist.

The sun was low and it seemed an evening walk,
But other feet were walking too quickly on my track.

When I reached where they had paused I found them gone.
I turned away and groped about for stones,
To keep my balance on the slippery ground.

--- v43_poetry_stephen_crane_01 stephen_crane ---
The Night-Comer

The night-comer, the night-comber,
the night-walker, the midnight-cramer;
He who walks in his sleep, he who lies awake all night
With arms akimbo and eyes distended wide--
Who are you? What have you done or seen to make your
body so tense? Who are you that do not rest, but stalk
through the streets? What is it you seek here at this hour?
You must come back to bed again: it is time for sleep.
